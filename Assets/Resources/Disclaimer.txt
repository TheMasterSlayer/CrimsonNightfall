CrimsonNightfall contains flashing lights, loud sounds, sudden audio, and horror imagery.

Photosensitive players and players sensitive to frightening content should use discretion.

Tips:
Having trouble finding the items needed in game or understanding what to do to win and survive? 
Make sure to utilize the crouch button, CTRL, in order to get down and search lower areas as a key or useful item may
be sitting on the floor, the bottom of a rack, a chair seat, and more.

Do you keep dying to the entity?
Try to crouch behind objects to be out of its field-of-view, or listen for its footsteps or the opening of doors.
Of course, the best survival trick is to hide in closets, but make sure to do it optimally.

Developer note to players:
This is my first personal videogame creation project, and I've made sure to thoroughly test it so that there aren't any game
breaking features present within gameplay. Personally as a gamer, I do try to find bugs in games, so I would expect some of you to try and do the same.

Lastly, there is only so much playtesting that can be done with the "Chaos" mode. It is clear that objects will fly, shift, and
ultimately be displaced from their current position, potentially making a gameplay impossible. This gamemode is only for those who enjoy a crazy challenge and maybe found "Normal" mode to be too easy.

There are a few easter eggs in the game. If you like easter eggs make sure to be on the search for them.
