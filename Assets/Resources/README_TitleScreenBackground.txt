Place your title-screen image(s) in this folder and name them:

TitleScreenBackground.png
TitleScreenBackground2.png
TitleScreenBackground3.png
...up to TitleScreenBackground8.png

In the Unity Inspector, set its Texture Type to "Sprite (2D and UI)".
The first two images will appear in the top and bottom halves of the right side of the title screen.
If you add more than two, the menu will rotate through them like a slideshow.
